# Acme Corp Employee Handbook — Addendum A (effective 2025-07-01)

This addendum updates and supersedes specific sections of the Employee Handbook v3.0 where noted. All other sections remain unchanged.

## Addendum 1: Updated Parental Leave (supersedes Section 2.3)

Effective 2025-07-01, parental leave is extended as follows:

Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of paid parental leave at 100% base salary (increased from 12 weeks). Secondary caregivers are entitled to 6 weeks of paid parental leave at 100% base salary (increased from 4 weeks).

Leave may now be taken in two blocks within 18 months of the child's birth or adoption date (previously a single continuous block within 12 months was required). The 30 days' advance notice requirement remains unchanged.

## Addendum 2: Updated Home-Office Equipment Allowance (supersedes Section 3.2)

Effective 2025-07-01, the one-time home-office setup allowance for eligible remote employees is increased from $300 to $500. Employees who received the previous $300 allowance after 2024-01-01 may claim the $200 difference by submitting an Equipment Allowance Top-Up Request to HR before 2025-12-31.

All other terms of Section 3.2 (company ownership of equipment, return upon termination) remain unchanged.

## Addendum 3: New — Remote Work Days Increase (extends Section 3.1)

Effective 2025-07-01, eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week (increased from 2 days per week), subject to manager approval. Core hours (Section 3.3) remain unchanged.

## Addendum 4: New — Acceptable Use of AI Tools

Employees may use company-approved AI tools (e.g., internal Claude Code deployment, approved LLM assistants) for drafting, coding, and research tasks, subject to the following:

1. Confidential or customer data must never be pasted into a non-approved third-party AI tool.
2. All AI-generated code must be reviewed by the employee before submission, per Section 6 (Code of Conduct) accountability standards.
3. Use of unapproved AI tools for handling PII or financial data is treated as a Code of Conduct violation and may be escalated to the ethics hotline described in Section 6.1.

## Addendum 5: New — Bereavement Leave Extension for Pregnancy Loss

Effective 2025-07-01, bereavement leave (Section 2.4) is extended to explicitly include up to 5 paid days for pregnancy loss or stillbirth, available to either parent, in addition to the existing categories in Section 2.4.
