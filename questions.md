# Sample Questions

Try these against `query.py` (see README.md for how to run). Organized by
what each category demonstrates.

## Basic facts (should answer correctly, one clean citation)
- How many days of paid sick leave do employees get per year?
- What is the probation period for new hires?
- How many days of paid annual leave do full-time employees accrue?
- What is the annual performance bonus cap?
- How much notice do individual contributors need to give when resigning?
- What is the meal allowance for international business travel?

## Addendum supersedes handbook (tests whether it prefers the newer policy)
- How many weeks of paid parental leave does a primary caregiver get?
- How many weeks of paid parental leave does a secondary caregiver get?
- What is the home-office equipment allowance?
- How many days per week can eligible employees work remotely?
- Can parental leave be split into two blocks?

## Addendum-only content (not in the base handbook at all)
- Can an employee paste customer data into an unapproved AI tool?
- Is bereavement leave available for pregnancy loss or stillbirth?

## Should refuse — genuinely not in the documents
- What is the company's policy on stock options?
- Does the company offer a gym membership stipend?
- What is the office policy on bringing pets to work?

## Multi-hop / requires combining two sections
- A new employee is 45 days into their job and wants to start working remotely 2 days a week — is that allowed, and if not, when?
- What's the maximum combined extra compensation, as a percent of salary, from the performance bonus and retirement match together?
- What is the maximum severance pay for someone with 10 years of service?

## Edge cases worth trying (known to be shaky — good for a write-up)
- How much bereavement leave do I get if I lose a sibling?
- If my spouse and I both work here and have a baby, can we both take parental leave at the same time?

## Suggested demo order
1. One basic fact
2. One addendum-supersedes case
3. One refusal
4. The probation/remote-work multi-hop question

This sequence hits correctness, citation, refusal, and reasoning in under a minute.
