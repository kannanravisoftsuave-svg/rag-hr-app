# Acme Corp Employee Handbook (v3.0, effective 2025-01-01)

## Section 1: Employment Basics

### 1.1 Work Hours
Standard work hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday, with a one-hour unpaid lunch break. Employees may request flexible start times between 7:00 AM and 10:00 AM with manager approval, provided they complete 8 working hours per day.

### 1.2 Probation Period
All new hires undergo a 90-day probation period. During this period, either party may terminate employment with 7 days' written notice. Performance reviews occur at day 30 and day 75 of probation.

### 1.3 Employment Classification
Employees are classified as Full-Time (35+ hours/week), Part-Time (under 35 hours/week), or Contractor. Only Full-Time employees are eligible for the benefits described in Section 4.

## Section 2: Leave Policy

### 2.1 Annual Leave
Full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued at 1.5 days per completed month of service. Unused leave up to 5 days may be carried over to the following year; any excess is forfeited on December 31.

### 2.2 Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry over between years.

### 2.3 Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 12 weeks of paid parental leave at 100% base salary. Secondary caregivers are entitled to 4 weeks of paid parental leave. Leave must be taken within 12 months of the child's birth or adoption date and requires 30 days' advance notice where practicable.

### 2.4 Bereavement Leave
Employees may take up to 5 paid days of bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling), and up to 2 paid days for extended family.

## Section 3: Remote Work Policy

### 3.1 Eligibility
Employees who have completed their 90-day probation period and whose role does not require physical presence (e.g., front-desk, warehouse) are eligible to work remotely up to 2 days per week, subject to manager approval.

### 3.2 Equipment
The company provides a laptop and a one-time home-office setup allowance of $300 for eligible remote employees. Equipment remains company property and must be returned upon termination of employment.

### 3.3 Core Hours
Remote employees must be available and responsive between 11:00 AM and 4:00 PM local time on days they work remotely, regardless of their chosen start time.

## Section 4: Compensation & Benefits

### 4.1 Health Insurance
Full-time employees and their dependents are eligible for company-subsidized health insurance starting on the first day of the month following 30 days of employment. The company covers 80% of the premium for employees and 50% for dependents.

### 4.2 Retirement Contributions
The company matches employee 401(k)/retirement contributions up to 4% of base salary, vested immediately.

### 4.3 Performance Bonus
Eligible employees may receive an annual discretionary performance bonus of up to 10% of base salary, paid in Q1 following the review period, subject to company and individual performance.

## Section 5: Expense Reimbursement

### 5.1 Travel Expenses
Business travel expenses (airfare, lodging, ground transport) must be pre-approved by a manager for amounts exceeding $500. Reimbursement requests must be submitted within 30 days of the expense with itemized receipts.

### 5.2 Meal Allowance
Employees traveling on company business are entitled to a meal allowance of $60/day domestically and $85/day internationally, without requiring itemized receipts below these thresholds.

## Section 6: Code of Conduct

### 6.1 Anti-Harassment
Acme Corp maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward harassment or discrimination based on race, gender, age, religion, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic. Violations should be reported to HR or via the anonymous ethics hotline and will be investigated within 10 business days.

### 6.2 Conflict of Interest
Employees must disclose any outside employment, board membership, or financial interest that could reasonably create a conflict with Acme Corp's business interests, using the annual Conflict of Interest Disclosure form.

## Section 7: Termination Policy

### 7.1 Voluntary Resignation
Employees resigning voluntarily must provide 2 weeks' written notice for individual contributors and 4 weeks' notice for managers and above.

### 7.2 Severance
Employees terminated without cause after completing probation are eligible for severance pay of 2 weeks' base salary per full year of service, capped at 16 weeks.

## Section 8: Workplace Safety

### 8.1 Incident Reporting
All workplace injuries or safety incidents, regardless of severity, must be reported to a manager and HR within 24 hours using the Incident Report Form.
